# Nimrod Ogachi

**Lifecycle marketing, CRM and retention for DTC brands.**

I’m the founder of **Repeat Foundry**, based in Nairobi, Kenya. My work focuses on helping brands earn the next purchase through useful customer communication and clear lifecycle journeys.

## What I focus on

- **Email and SMS:** welcome sequences, abandoned-cart flows and post-purchase education.
- **Customer retention:** segmentation, second-purchase journeys and win-back planning.
- **CRM and automation:** organized customer data, follow-up workflows and measurement plans.
- **Klaviyo:** translating customer-journey strategy into practical flow designs.
